About

Learning from data that never stops.

I hold a PhD in Computer Science from the University of Waikato in New Zealand, completed in 2025. I develop machine learning methods for streaming data, where models need to learn continuously as the world changes.

My broader interests include concept drift, online continual learning, uncertainty quantification, dynamic ensemble selection, and open-source tools that make stream learning accessible. I also apply these methods to energy pricing and electromyography (EMG) signals.

Research & open source

Current areas of contribution

01

Adaptive learning

Incremental models that respond to concept drift and learn continuously from non-stationary data.

02

Uncertainty quantification

Fully incremental prediction intervals and evaluation methods for reliable streaming regression.

03

Dynamic ensembles

Adaptive member selection that balances accuracy and diversity as classification streams evolve.

04

Applied stream learning

Real-time methods for New Zealand energy pricing and domain-shift detection in EMG signals.

05

CapyMOA

An open-source Python library for efficient data stream learning and online continual learning.

06

Massive Online Analysis
